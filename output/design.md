# Halloween Storefront Design

- Reframed the home hero as a moving film-noir night scene using a real Yale hoodie product photo, moon haze, grain, and warm spotlight treatment. The cinematic mood makes the collection feel seasonal while the featured image still links straight to its product page.
- Added original Handsome Dan-inspired Halloween artwork in a Yale scarf and witch hat as a decorative accent. It adds campus-specific Halloween character without replacing real merchandise imagery.
- Added a one-time, dismissible “Spooky Good Prices” popup that leads directly to Products. Its copy promises no fabricated markdowns, so it attracts attention without misleading shoppers.
- Shifted the palette to ink, oxblood, and lamplight while keeping navigation, prices, product cards, and primary shopping buttons clear and readable.
